Bryan's Writing Tips & Structure

  • Informal Email Strategy: Always address all three prompt bullet points in separate paragraphs. Memorise at least 3 advanced vocabulary items and 3 linking phrases to improve fluency.
  • Word Count Control: Keep tightly within the specified range (120–160 or 150–200 words) to reduce chances of grammar errors and keep the answer focused.
