✍️ English as a Second Language

Syllabus Code: 0510 / 0511

Bryan's Writing Tips & Structure

  • Informal Email Strategy: Always address all three prompt bullet points in separate paragraphs. Use varied linking words (e.g., Furthermore, On top of that, As a result) rather than basic connectors.
  • Word Count Control: Keep tightly within 120–160 words so examiners don't dock marks for overlength.

Featured Model Answer: School Trip Email

Task Prompt (Museum Trip):
Write an email to a friend about a recent school trip to a museum in another city (120–160 words). Must include:
  • Explain what you did in class to prepare for the museum trip
  • Describe what you liked about the museum trip
  • Tell your friend about a problem with the museum trip

Sample Response:

Dear Ahmad, How’s it going? I really missed our hangouts! It’s been a while. I hope your whirlwind of studies comes to an end soon. Guess what? I went to the Grand Egyptian Museum last Thursday again, but it was a school trip this time. We arrived at school extra early so our history teacher could give us an overview of what to expect. They also gave us small maps of the museum, which came in handy when we got separated. Honestly, my friends weren’t too excited about a museum trip at first, but we all agreed it was awesome at the end! Our guide was so friendly and knowledgeable. Plus, we had the entire place to ourselves! The best part was seeing the colossal statue of Ramses II—it was so majestic! We were really enjoying ourselves, until the teachers announced it was over. An hour and a half didn’t give us the full experience at all, and we missed some exhibits, which was very disappointing. Anyways, I should go now. Don’t worry, I’ll send you the photos tonight! Lots of love, [Your Name]

Why This Scores High Marks:

  • ✅ Covers All 3 Bullet Points: Mentions prep (history overview & maps), likes (friendly guide, Ramses II statue), and problem (too short, missed exhibits).
  • ✅ Natural Informal Tone: Great friendly phrases ("How’s it going?", "Guess what?", "Lots of love").
  • ✅ Rich Vocabulary: Excellent word choices like whirlwind of studies, came in handy, colossal, and majestic.
