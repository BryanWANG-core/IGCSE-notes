📝 Paper 2: MCQ Overview & Strategy

Key techniques for tackling 0610 Paper 2 multiple-choice questions:

  • Elimination Method: Rule out two obviously incorrect options immediately before focusing on subtle differences between the remaining choices.
  • Key Definitions: Watch for strict definitions regarding osmosis, active transport, and enzyme denaturation.
  • Data & Graphs: Pay close attention to axis labels and units on rate-of-reaction graphs.

📄 Paper 4: Theory Notes & High-Yield Topics

Structured notes covering core syllabus units:

  • Cell Structure & Organization: Plant vs. animal cell organelle functions.
  • Enzymes & Biological Molecules: Temperature and pH effects on active site specificity.
  • Transport in Plants & Humans: Xylem/phloem, transpiration pull, and circulatory pathways.

🔬 Paper 6: Alternative to Practical (ATP)

Essential practical skills and food test summaries:

  • Food Tests: Benedict's (reducing sugars), Iodine (starch), Biuret (proteins), Ethanol emulsion (lipids).
  • Biological Drawing Rules: Use clear continuous lines, no shading/cross-hatching, label lines drawn with a ruler touching the target structure.
  • Variables: Clear identification of independent, dependent, and controlled variables in experiment designs.
