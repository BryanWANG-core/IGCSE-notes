⚠️ Frequently Tested Misconceptions

❌ Trap 1: "Enzymes die or are killed at high temperatures"
Correct Concept: Enzymes are not living organisms; they cannot "die." At high temperatures, their active sites change shape permanently (they become denatured), preventing substrates from fitting. At low temperatures, they are simply inactive due to low kinetic energy.
❌ Trap 2: "Osmosis is just water moving from high to low concentration"
Correct Concept: Always specify water potential! Osmosis is the net movement of water molecules from a region of higher water potential (dilute solution) to a region of lower water potential (concentrated solution) through a partially permeable membrane.
❌ Trap 3: "Respiration is the same as breathing"
Correct Concept: Breathing (ventilation) is the mechanical muscle movement moving air in and out of lungs. Respiration is the chemical reaction inside cells that breaks down nutrient molecules (like glucose) to release energy.
❌ Trap 4: "Arteries always carry oxygenated blood"
Correct Concept: Arteries carry blood away from the heart. The pulmonary artery is the major exception—it carries deoxygenated blood from the right ventricle to the lungs.

📌 Must-Remember MCQ Rules

🔬 Magnification Formula

Remember I = A × M (Image size = Actual size × Magnification).

Check units carefully! Always convert millimetres (mm) to micrometres (µm) by multiplying by 1,000 before calculating.

🌱 Plant Transport Distinctions

Xylem: Unidirectional (roots to leaves), dead tissue, carries water + mineral salts, supported by lignin.

Phloem: Bidirectional, living tissue, carries sucrose + amino acids (translocation).

🧪 Food Test Color Triggers

• Reducing Sugar (Benedict's): Blue ➔ Green ➔ Yellow ➔ Brick Red (requires heat!)

• Starch (Iodine): Yellow-brown ➔ Blue-black

• Protein (Biuret): Blue ➔ Purple/Violet

• Fats (Ethanol Emulsion): Clear ➔ Milky-white emulsion
